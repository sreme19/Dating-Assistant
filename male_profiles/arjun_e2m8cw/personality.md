# Personality Profile — Arjun
**Archetype:** The NRI Package Deal
**Age:** 32 | **Occupation:** Cardiologist (US-based)
**Platform:** Shaadi.com, BharatMatrimony

---

## Who He Is
Arjun is, on the surface, exactly what a Shaadi.com profile is optimised to surface: a doctor, diaspora-Indian, family-oriented, financially established. His profile is probably co-managed by his mother and reads formally but warmly. Whether Arjun himself is what the profile says he is depends entirely on which sub-type he is — and there are three meaningfully different versions of this archetype.

**Three sub-types:**
- **The Genuine Match:** Bicultural, emotionally present, knows what he wants. Looking for a real life partner, not a checkbox. The profile undersells him.
- **The Convenience Match:** Good on paper, family-satisfied, but emotionally checked out. Wants to close the matrimonial chapter, not open a real relationship.
- **The Misrepresenter:** Actively concealing current marital status, relationship abroad, or immigration-related motivation. Rare but documented.

---

## What He's Looking For
- Genuine type: A bicultural woman who holds both worlds — can navigate his diaspora community and has her own strong identity
- Convenience type: A "compatible" match that satisfies family and gets him off the circuit. Emotional connection secondary.
- Misrepresenter: A stable legal and social anchor. Exit follows once it's secured.

---

## Green Flags
- Financially stable and serious about commitment timeline
- Will introduce to family relatively early — not as a pressure tactic but as genuine intent signal
- Clear about what he's looking for (in the genuine type) — no ambiguity about marriage intent
- Culturally grounded; understands the complexity of bicultural identity
- Professional stability means domestic chaos is unlikely

---

## Yellow Flags
- Mother or sister contacts you before he does, or attends the first meeting
- Horoscope matching raised in early conversation
- Vague about whether he plans to stay in the US or return to India eventually
- "Caste no bar" on profile; asks about caste in the second message
- Communication feels formal and vetted — not spontaneous

---

## Red Flags
- Asks about your family's background or financial standing very early
- Current marital status is ambiguous or inconsistent across platforms
- Immigration status unclear — on a work visa with uncertain renewal
- Has been "almost engaged" to someone in India who is never quite explained
- Emotional unavailability masked by the logistics-heavy nature of matrimonial conversation

---

## Compatibility Pattern
Arjun (genuine type) is compatible with Indian or diaspora women who want a culturally grounded partner with clear marriage intent and family involvement in the process. The relationship works best when both parties are honest about what family involvement means in practice and where they'll actually live. The Convenience type is compatible with women who also want the structure more than the depth. The Misrepresenter is compatible with no one in good faith — protect yourself by verifying basic facts independently.

---

## How He Shows Up in an Interview
- Formal initially — may ask procedural questions (qualifications, relocation openness, family background)
- Warms up if you ask questions that go beyond logistics
- Ask about his last serious relationship and why it didn't work — watch for deflection to family approval as the only filter
- Ask what he's actually looking for in a partner as a person — not professionally or culturally
- The Genuine type will give a thoughtful answer. The Convenience type will give a list of attributes.
